## Not available

* **Opening lines, line history, timestamps of odds, cross-book quotes**: not in nflverse; The Odds API history is a paid product; The Prediction Tracker archive (Deadeye's training source) returns HTTP 403 (Cloudflare) from this environment.
* **Archived weather forecasts**: Open-Meteo's historical-forecast archive starts in 2022; not fetched. Weather results are observed-weather upper bounds.
* **Deadeye's logged forecasts** (`data/acql.db`): git-ignored, not in the repository.
* **PFF / Next Gen Stats tracking, coverage charting before 2016**: licensed or unavailable.
