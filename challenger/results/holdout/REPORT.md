## What this does and does not show

- A difference whose interval includes zero is not evidence either way; one hot season is not a verdict.
- The Deadeye proxy is Deadeye's own historical stand-in, not the live engine (market + computer-model consensus), which cannot be replayed before 2026 here because the Prediction Tracker archives are not available to this environment.
- Market baselines and `deadeye_core_closing` use closing lines, which post-date the ACQL deadline.
- Challenger's play-by-play EPA comes from today's nflfastR model, fitted on many seasons, including seasons after the games being predicted - a mild retrospective-data advantage (see the no_epa variant).
